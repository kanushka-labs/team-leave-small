# Team Leave

## Problem Statement

Employees today track leave requests and balances through ad hoc means —
spreadsheets, email, chat — which makes it hard to see an accurate balance or
history, and hard for managers to find and act on pending requests from their
team.

## Solution

Team Leave is a small web app where employees request leave (dates, type,
reason) and see their balance and request history, while managers see their
team's pending requests and approve or reject them with a comment.

## Actors

- Employee — requests leave, and views their own leave balance and request history.
- Manager — reviews their team's pending leave requests, and approves or rejects each one with a comment.
